PlayStoreScreenshotMaker

Free. Fast. Frame your store screenshots in seconds.

Turn your screenshots into device-framed Play Store screenshots at 2160×3840, entirely in your browser. Upload screenshots, pick a device and background, preview every combination, then export individual PNGs or a single ZIP.

  • 145 device frames — iPhones, iPads, Pixels, Galaxy phones & tablets
  • Pixel-perfect compositing: cover-crop + mask clip + authentic frame overlay
  • Editable solid / gradient / image backgrounds
  • Worker-based batch export that never janks the UI

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Space toggle selection of the focused thumbnail or device
  • Ctrl/Cmd + Z undo
  • A select all · T toggle theme · Esc close
  • ↑↓←→ navigate grids
